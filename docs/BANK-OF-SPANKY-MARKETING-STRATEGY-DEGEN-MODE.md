# BANK OF SPANKY — MARKETING STRATEGY & DEGEN MODE

**Strategy version:** 1.0  
**Documented:** 2026-11-26  
**Status:** Active strategic reference  
**Brand:** Bank of Spanky (BoS)  
**Primary channel:** X (Twitter)  
**Supporting channels:** Website, NFT collection, Discord/community, video and visual campaigns  
**Content planning:** Weekly X content planner  
**Publishing mode:** Content is planned and reviewed before publishing; no unverified automatic publishing assumptions.

---

## 1. Strategy in One Sentence

Build BANK OF SPANKY into a recognizable NFT and Web3 brand by combining institutional financial satire, degen humor, collectible character culture, cinematic storytelling, and community participation — turning the bank into a memorable brand universe rather than another NFT project shouting for attention.

## 2. Marketing Philosophy

BANK OF SPANKY is not supposed to look, sound, or behave like a conventional NFT project.

The brand takes the visual language of traditional financial institutions — vaults, marble architecture, gold, pillars, ledgers, bank doors, prestige and authority — and mixes it with the absurdity, humor, irreverence and unpredictability of crypto culture.

The contrast is the brand.

BoS should create the impression of a serious institution while delivering unserious, self-aware crypto energy. The visual presentation can feel premium, cinematic and powerful, while the social voice stays playful, rebellious, sharp and relatable.

The objective is not simply to advertise NFTs. It is to establish a recognizable identity, develop a memorable brand universe, attract attention, encourage community participation and give people reasons to follow the project's evolution.

### Core brand formula

**Institutional aesthetics + crypto satire + SPANKY character identity + NFT culture + community-driven storytelling.**

## 3. Primary Marketing Objectives

1. **Build brand recognition:** Make BANK OF SPANKY instantly recognizable through its recurring visual identity, language, characters and campaign concepts.
2. **Establish a recognizable voice:** Develop a distinctive X personality that feels crypto-native, funny, self-aware and consistent.
3. **Create attention through creative storytelling:** Use cinematic imagery, videos, memes, campaign scenes and narrative-driven content to stop people scrolling.
4. **Grow an engaged community:** Encourage conversations, participation, feedback, community contributions and genuine relationships.
5. **Communicate the NFT ecosystem:** Make the collection, holder benefits, website, community and project developments easy to understand without constant sales messaging.
6. **Build long-term brand equity:** Create recurring campaign concepts, memorable characters, visual assets and stories that can evolve over time.
7. **Convert attention into participation:** Give interested people clear paths to explore the project, collection, website and community.

## 4. Brand Identity & Positioning

### The Bank of Spanky identity

BANK OF SPANKY presents itself as a fictional institution built for the unpredictable world of crypto and NFT culture.

The bank is a creative universe, not a traditional financial institution. Its visual language borrows from banking and financial architecture to create a distinctive satirical identity.

### Visual identity

The primary visual language should consistently emphasize:

- Black marble and dark architectural environments.
- Gold pillars, frames, vaults, doors, coins and institutional details.
- Premium lighting and cinematic shadows.
- Symmetrical architecture and monumental composition.
- Reflections, glass, ledgers and subtle digital or blockchain-inspired geometry.
- The SPANKY character integrated naturally into environments.
- Premium editorial and cinematic campaign photography.
- NFT artwork presented as collectible art rather than generic promotional graphics.

### Visual contrast

The visual presentation should feel expensive, powerful, architectural and serious.

The actual messaging can be ridiculous, cheeky, sarcastic, provocative and unmistakably crypto-native.

That contrast is one of the strongest creative assets of the brand.

## 5. Content Pillars

These pillars guide the weekly content schedule. They should be mixed organically rather than used as rigid quotas.

### A. DEGEN CULTURE & CRYPTO HUMOR

The foundation of BoS's social personality.

Explore the relatable chaos of crypto culture: market emotions, FOMO, missed opportunities, chart watching, wallet anxiety, unrealistic expectations, diamond hands, meme culture and the absurdity of Web3.

**Content examples:**
- GM/GN posts with personality.
- Relatable crypto situations.
- Sarcastic observations about market behavior.
- Crypto memes and humorous scenarios.
- Unexpected one-liners.
- Community jokes and playful commentary.
- Self-aware NFT culture references.

**Execution:** Make the audience laugh, relate or want to reply. Avoid generic recycled crypto jokes.

### B. BANK OF SPANKY BRAND UNIVERSE

Build the fictional institution into a recognizable world.

Explore the bank's visual identity, architecture, fictional institutional imagery, vaults, pillars, ledgers, gallery exhibitions, premium collectible displays and creative environments.

**Content examples:**
- Cinematic scenes inside the Bank.
- SPANKY exploring the institution.
- The vault, bank lobby, gallery or institutional environment.
- Visual campaign concepts.
- Premium black-and-gold artwork.
- Recurring visual motifs and campaign storytelling.

**Execution:** Each visual should feel like it belongs to the same universe. Consistency is more important than randomly generating unrelated images.

### C. OG SPANKY — CHARACTER STORYTELLING

OG SPANKY is a recognizable visual anchor for the brand.

The character should appear in creative scenes, campaign visuals, videos, memes and narrative moments that reinforce the Bank of Spanky's personality.

**Content examples:**
- SPANKY interacting with bank environments.
- SPANKY inspecting paintings, ledgers or collectible displays.
- SPANKY in humorous crypto situations.
- Cinematic character-based promotional scenes.
- Animated campaign moments.
- Character-driven visual jokes.
- SPANKY reacting to crypto culture.

**Execution:** Preserve the recognizable character design when using reference images. Avoid treating SPANKY as a disposable character that changes appearance from one campaign to the next.

### D. NFT & COLLECTIBLE CULTURE

Communicate the collectible side of the project while participating in broader NFT culture.

**Content examples:**
- Collectible artwork showcases.
- NFT culture commentary.
- Collecting and digital ownership discussions.
- Artwork presentation and gallery-style campaigns.
- Community collection moments.
- Creative NFT storytelling.
- Relevant Web3 conversations.

**Execution:** Do not turn every collectible post into a minting or purchasing pitch. Art, culture, humor and storytelling should have independent value.

### E. THE NOT YOUR MONEY CAMPAIGN & BRAND NARRATIVES

Use recurring campaign concepts to create memorable marketing moments and recognizable storylines.

Campaigns should be more than isolated graphics. They should combine visuals, copy, video, recurring motifs and social interactions into cohesive creative sequences.

**The NOT YOUR MONEY concept** is an example of a campaign direction that can use dark humor, financial symbolism, institutional visuals, dramatic narration and memorable messaging to establish a strong campaign identity.

**Potential execution formats:**
- Short cinematic videos.
- Campaign trailers.
- Character-based scenes.
- Visual storytelling sequences.
- Recurring campaign slogans.
- Short-form social clips.
- Supporting memes and reactions.
- Campaign-related community interactions.

**Execution:** Campaign messaging should remain creative and clearly branded. Avoid implying financial guarantees, investment outcomes or factual claims that are not supported.

### F. HOLDER BENEFITS & ECOSYSTEM

Communicate what the NFT ecosystem offers and what is being developed.

Existing benefit-oriented messaging includes concepts such as:

- Private Discord access.
- Exclusive learning content and courses.
- Planned exclusive merch drops and merch airdrops.
- Holder-oriented experiences and community access.

**Content examples:**
- Benefit explainers.
- Holder access messaging.
- Community updates.
- Feature showcases.
- Website walkthroughs.
- Educational content previews.
- Merch concepts and drop previews.
- Ecosystem updates.

**Execution:** Clearly distinguish active benefits from planned benefits. Do not present future features, planned airdrops or upcoming releases as already available.

### G. COMMUNITY & CONVERSATION

The community should be part of the project's development and marketing, not merely the audience receiving promotional posts.

**Content examples:**
- Questions about crypto and NFT culture.
- Community opinions.
- Creative suggestions.
- Feedback about the website and ecosystem.
- Discussions about artwork, content and future ideas.
- Community spotlights.
- Interactive posts.
- Reply-driven engagement.

**Execution:** Ask questions that people genuinely want to answer. Make participation easy and welcome newcomers without assuming they already know the project.

### H. BUILDING & PROJECT PROGRESS

Show the evolution of Bank of Spanky through creative work, website updates, content production, campaign development and ecosystem progress.

**Content examples:**
- Website improvements.
- New creative assets.
- Campaign production.
- Videos being developed.
- Collection-related updates.
- Community initiatives.
- Behind-the-scenes creative work.
- Progress updates.

**Execution:** Share real progress. Make the process interesting without exaggerating accomplishments or presenting unfinished work as complete.

## 6. X CONTENT & POSTING APPROACH

X is the primary social platform for brand voice, crypto culture participation, community interaction, campaign distribution and ongoing project visibility.

The feed should not feel like a repetitive sequence of promotional announcements.

Instead, it should feel like the Bank of Spanky is an active participant in crypto culture.

### Posting principles

- Maintain a consistent presence throughout the week.
- Mix humor, brand storytelling, visuals, campaign content, community interaction and project updates.
- Use the weekly planner to maintain consistency and prevent content repetition.
- Create original content rather than relying exclusively on recycled memes.
- Use timely content when relevant.
- Keep promotional posts balanced with entertaining and conversational posts.
- Make every post serve a recognizable purpose.
- Use different formats to avoid feed fatigue.

### Recommended content mix

The following is directional rather than a rigid percentage allocation:

- **Entertainment:** crypto humor, memes and relatable degen experiences.
- **Brand identity:** Bank of Spanky universe and SPANKY character content.
- **Visual storytelling:** campaign imagery, cinematic scenes and video.
- **Community:** questions, discussions, feedback and interactions.
- **Ecosystem:** NFTs, holder benefits and project developments.
- **Culture:** relevant NFT, Web3 and crypto conversations.

The weekly schedule should reflect this variety.

## 7. BRAND VOICE — BANK OF SPANKY DEGEN MODE

### Personality

- Degen.
- Funny.
- Sarcastic.
- Confident.
- Self-aware.
- Playful.
- Slightly chaotic.
- Creative.
- Crypto-native.
- Occasionally provocative.
- Community-friendly.

### Writing rules

- Write in short, punchy, natural English.
- Prefer conversational language over formal marketing language.
- Make the first line interesting.
- Use humor when it fits.
- Keep posts easy to read while scrolling.
- Use crypto slang naturally, not artificially.
- Use emojis selectively.
- Avoid unnecessary corporate vocabulary.
- Avoid overexplaining jokes.
- Use questions when they genuinely invite participation.
- Vary hooks, sentence structures, jokes and formats.
- Maintain the Bank of Spanky personality without making every post sound identical.

### Examples of the tone

**GM energy:**

GM GM, SPANKY squads. The bank is open. Your financial decisions are still questionable. 🏦

**Crypto humor:**

My financial advisor is a green candle I haven't seen in 3 months.

**Brand personality:**

Welcome to the Bank of Spanky. We take the institution seriously. The institution does not take us seriously.

**Community engagement:**

Be honest. Did you join crypto for the technology, the money, or the memes that permanently altered your brain?

These examples illustrate the tone, not reusable templates that should be repeated every week.

## 8. VISUAL MARKETING & CREATIVE PRODUCTION

Visual content is a major component of Bank of Spanky's brand recognition.

The goal is to develop a consistent visual universe rather than generate unrelated images that happen to use the same character.

### Creative direction

- Premium black-and-gold environments.
- Cinematic compositions.
- Architectural storytelling.
- Monumental financial institution imagery.
- Character integration.
- Gallery and collectible presentation.
- Humor through visual contrast.
- High-quality editorial imagery.
- Campaign-specific visual themes.

### Image production guidelines

When generating campaign images:

1. Use the approved SPANKY character reference.
2. Preserve the character's identity and design.
3. Match the Bank of Spanky's visual aesthetic.
4. Use coherent environments and lighting.
5. Avoid unnecessary typography inside generated imagery.
6. Avoid inconsistent character redesigns.
7. Create visuals that can be reused across posts, campaigns, website sections and promotional assets when appropriate.

### Video strategy

Videos should be used as storytelling and attention-generating assets.

Potential formats include:

- Cinematic campaign trailers.
- Character animations.
- Short promotional clips.
- Narrative-driven videos.
- Humorous scenes.
- NFT and brand universe showcases.
- Campaign sequences.
- Website promotional videos.

Videos should have a clear creative concept, recognizable branding and a strong opening hook.

## 9. COMMUNITY GROWTH & ENGAGEMENT

The goal is to attract people who genuinely enjoy the brand and want to participate in its evolution.

### Engagement priorities

- Participate in relevant crypto and NFT conversations.
- Reply to community members with personality.
- Engage with relevant creators and projects naturally.
- Use campaign content to create conversation.
- Ask questions that generate useful feedback.
- Encourage creative participation.
- Highlight community contributions when appropriate.
- Avoid spam, forced tagging and copy-paste replies.

### Community feedback loop

Community feedback should inform future content, creative decisions and project priorities.

Use feedback to identify:

- What people enjoy.
- Which campaign concepts resonate.
- Which features are confusing.
- What content people want.
- What improvements are suggested.
- Which community initiatives generate interest.

Treat responses as qualitative evidence. Do not exaggerate feedback or present a few responses as representative of the entire community.

## 10. CAMPAIGN OPERATING MODEL

Major campaigns should be planned as cohesive creative sequences rather than single promotional posts.

### Campaign structure

**Phase 1 — Concept**

Define the campaign theme, message, visual identity and desired audience reaction.

**Phase 2 — Creative production**

Prepare images, character scenes, video assets, copy and supporting campaign materials.

**Phase 3 — Introduction**

Introduce the campaign concept through a strong visual or narrative hook.

**Phase 4 — Expansion**

Develop the campaign through follow-up posts, creative variations, memes, video moments and community interaction.

**Phase 5 — Engagement**

Encourage conversations, reactions, feedback and community participation.

**Phase 6 — Wrap-up**

Conclude the campaign, share relevant outcomes and preserve useful assets for future marketing.

Campaigns should be flexible. Not every campaign needs all six phases or the same duration.

## 11. CONTENT PLANNER & WEEKLY OPERATING WORKFLOW

The weekly X content planner is the operational foundation for maintaining consistency.

### Weekly planning principles

- Plan across the full seven-day week.
- Maintain a varied content mix.
- Avoid repetitive posts and recycled ideas.
- Use the planner to organize campaign sequences.
- Include relevant visual assets.
- Keep post timing aligned with the existing scheduler configuration.
- Review the week's content before publication.
- Preserve existing planner history.

### Weekly workflow

1. Review recent posts and content to identify repetition.
2. Review current crypto and NFT conversations when timely content is needed.
3. Identify campaign priorities for the upcoming week.
4. Select content across the established pillars.
5. Draft original posts with varied hooks and formats.
6. Prepare supporting visual assets.
7. Organize the posts in the weekly planner.
8. Review the full week for variety, consistency, accuracy and campaign alignment.
9. Approve content before publication.
10. Record actual publication outcomes.
11. Review performance and use observations to refine the next week.

The planner is a planning and organization tool. A scheduled draft must never be treated as a published post without confirmation.

## 12. PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Evaluate marketing using real data available from the platform and project channels.

### Key performance indicators

**Content performance**
- Impressions.
- Likes.
- Replies.
- Reposts.
- Engagement rate, when reliably calculated.
- Link clicks, where available.

**Community performance**
- Meaningful conversations.
- Relevant new followers.
- Community participation.
- Feedback received.
- Community activity.

**Campaign performance**
- Engagement across campaign posts.
- Audience reactions to campaign visuals.
- Video performance.
- Website traffic or link clicks, where tracked.
- Campaign-related community activity.

**Content quality**
- Which content pillars generate meaningful responses.
- Which formats perform consistently.
- Which hooks work best.
- Which posts feel repetitive.
- Which topics deserve follow-up.

### Review principles

- Use real platform data.
- Do not invent metrics.
- Do not interpret impressions alone as meaningful community growth.
- Compare content types where data is available.
- Look for patterns over time rather than judging one post in isolation.
- Refine the strategy based on evidence.

## 13. HARD EXCLUSIONS & BRAND TRUST RULES

- No generic corporate marketing voice.
- No repetitive NFT sales messaging.
- No fabricated project updates.
- No invented metrics, testimonials or community activity.
- No misleading claims about NFT ownership, benefits or features.
- No unsupported financial promises or guaranteed returns.
- No presenting planned features as live.
- No artificial engagement spam.
- No forced mentions or unrelated tagging.
- No automatic publishing assumptions.
- No deleting or overwriting historical planner records when generating new content.
- No inconsistent SPANKY character redesigns in reference-based campaigns.
- No unnecessary visual clutter or low-quality campaign assets.
