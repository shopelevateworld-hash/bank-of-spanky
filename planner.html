THE VOICE / BANK OF SPANKY

A rebellious institution. A serious idea. Zero boring posts.

Bank of Spanky blends luxury-bank theatre, crypto-native humor and a challenge to unequal monetary systems. The feed should entertain first, make people curious second, and explain the bigger vision through consistent, credible ideas.

Institutional swagger, vault language, “get access”, empire-building, announcements and the signature line: NOT YOUR MONEY.

Voice & writing rules

  • Short, punchy American English. Confident, cheeky and direct.
  • Use uppercase selectively for signature phrases: THE BANK, THE VAULT, SPANKY ARMY.
  • Mix sharp one-liners with occasional thoughtful whitepaper ideas.
  • Use emojis as punctuation, not decoration on every sentence.
  • Keep most posts to one clear idea and one action.
  • Sound like a brand with a point of view, not an automated shill account.

Trust, promotion & consistency

  • Never promise profits, token appreciation, guaranteed rewards or risk-free outcomes.
  • Separate live product utility from proposed roadmap and economic vision.
  • Do not call the existing financial system a Ponzi scheme; critique structural inequality precisely.
  • Promote NFTs, Discord and merch with accurate links and current details.
  • Use 0–2 relevant hashtags only when they add discovery value.
  • Tag accounts only when genuinely relevant; avoid repetitive tag spam.
  • Rotate pillars, formats, hooks and calls to action. Avoid repeating the same CTA back-to-back.
  • Invite replies with real questions; reply like a human.
